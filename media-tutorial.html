Media Sources Tutorial

Learn how to properly cite and reference videos, podcasts, and broadcasts in Harvard style

In-text Citations

▼

Media sources can include videos, podcasts, TV shows, and documentaries:

Video/Documentary: (Smith, 2021)

Podcast: (Johnson, 2021)

TV Programme: ("Programme Title", 2021)

Examples:

  • The documentary explores climate change (Smith, 2021).
  • As discussed in the podcast (Johnson, 2021).

YouTube Videos and Online Videos

▼

Format: Creator, A. (Year, Month Day). Video title [Video]. Platform. URL

Example: TED. (2021, April 15). The future of artificial intelligence [Video]. YouTube. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=example

Individual Creator: Smith, J. (2021, June 10). How to study effectively [Video]. YouTube. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=example

Podcasts

▼

Format: Host, A. (Host). (Year, Month Day). Episode title [Audio podcast episode]. In Podcast series name. Production Company.

Example: Rogan, J. (Host). (2021, March 20). AI and the future of work [Audio podcast episode]. In The Joe Rogan Experience. Spotify.

With URL: Add the URL at the end if available online.

TV Programmes and Documentaries

▼

TV Episode: Writer, A. (Writer), & Director, B. (Director). (Year, Month Day). Episode title [TV series episode]. In Producer, C. (Executive Producer), Series name. Network.

Documentary: Director, A. (Director). (Year). Documentary title [Film]. Production Company.

News Broadcast: Presenter, A. (Presenter). (Year, Month Day). News programme name [TV broadcast]. Network.
