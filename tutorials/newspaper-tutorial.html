Newspaper Referencing Tutorial

Learn how to properly cite and reference newspaper articles in Harvard style

In-text Citations

▼

Newspaper citations follow similar patterns to other sources:

With Author: (Smith, 2021)

Organisation as Author: (BBC, 2021)

No Author: ("Article title", 2021)

Examples:

  • Economic growth is slowing (Johnson, 2021).
  • The Guardian (2021) reports on climate initiatives.

Print Newspaper Articles

▼

Format: Author, A.A. (Year, Month Day). Article title. Newspaper Name, p. page number.

Example: Smith, J. (2021, March 15). Climate change accelerates. The Times, p. 4.

No Author: Climate summit begins. (2021, April 10). The Guardian, p. 1.

Note: The newspaper name is italicised

Online Newspaper Articles

▼

Format: Author, A.A. (Year, Month Day). Article title. Newspaper Name. URL

Example: Brown, M. (2021, June 20). Digital transformation in education. The Independent. https://www.independent.co.uk/news/education/digital-transformation

Note: Include the full URL for online newspaper articles. The newspaper name is italicised

Special Cases

▼

Editorial (No Author): Education funding cuts. (2021, May 15). The Times [Editorial], p. 12.

Letter to Editor: Smith, J. (2021, July 8). Response to climate article [Letter to the editor]. The Guardian, p. 15.

News Wire Services: Reuters. (2021, August 12). Global markets rise. Financial Times. URL
