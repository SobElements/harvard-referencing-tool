Website Referencing Tutorial

Learn how to properly cite and reference websites in Harvard style

In-text Citations (Direct Quotes)

▼

Website citations can vary depending on available information. Sometimes the Author of the work may not be available:

With Author: (Smith, 2021)

Organisation as Author: (BBC, 2021)

No Author: ("Article Title", 2021)

Examples:
Online education is growing (Smith, 2021)
The BBC (2021) reports increasing online learning

When quoting directly from a website, you typically use the same format as paraphrasing since websites usually don't have page numbers: "Online learning continues to grow" (Smith, 2021).
Note: If a website does include page numbers or paragraph numbers, you can include them, but this is rare for web content.

Reference List - With Author

▼

Format: Author Surname, Initial. (Year) Title of webpage. Website Name. Available at: URL (Accessed: Day Month Year).

Example: Smith, J. (2021) The future of online learning. Education Today. Available at: https://www.educationtoday.com/online-learning (Accessed: 15 March 2021).

Note: The website name should be in italics.

Reference List - Organisation/No Author

▼

Organisation as Author: BBC (2021) Technology in schools. BBC News. Available at: https://www.bbc.com/news/technology (Accessed: 10 April 2021).

No Author: The impact of social media (2021) Digital Trends. Available at: https://www.digitaltrends.com/social-media (Accessed: 20 May 2021).

Note: The website name should be in italics.
