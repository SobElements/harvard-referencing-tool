Book Referencing Tutorial

Learn how to properly cite and reference books in Harvard style

In-text Citations (Direct Quotes)

▼

When quoting directly from a book, include the author's surname, year of publication, and page number:

Format: (Author, Year, p. page number)

Example: "Knowledge is power" (Smith, 2020, p. 45).

If the author's name appears in your sentence: Smith (2020, p. 45) argues that "knowledge is power".

In-text Citations (Paraphrasing)

▼

When paraphrasing or summarising ideas from a book, include the author and year:

Format: (Author, Year)

Example: The concept of knowledge as a form of power has been widely discussed (Smith, 2020).

Alternative: Smith (2020) discusses the relationship between knowledge and power.

Reference List Entry

▼

Books that have been included in your text should also be included in a Reference List. Books in the reference list should follow this format:

Format: Author, A.A. (Year). Title of book. Publisher.

Example: Smith, J. (2020). The power of knowledge: A comprehensive guide. Academic Press.

Multiple Authors: Smith, J. & Jones, M. (2020). Title. Publisher.

Note: Book titles should be in italics, and only the first word of the title and subtitle (after a colon) should be capitalised, along with proper nouns.

Special Cases

▼

No Author: Start with the title: Oxford English Dictionary (2020). Publisher.

Edited Book: Editor, A. (Ed.). (Year). Title. Publisher.

Chapter in Edited Book: Author, A. (Year). Chapter title. In B. Editor (Ed.), Book title (pp. 1-20). Publisher.

Multiple Editions: Author, A. (Year). Title (2nd ed.). Publisher.
