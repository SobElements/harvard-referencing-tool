Reports and Government Documents Tutorial

Learn how to properly cite and reference reports and government publications in Harvard style

In-text Citations

▼

Government documents and reports are cited by the publishing organisation:

Government Department: (Department of Education, 2021)

Organisation Report: (World Bank, 2021)

Specific Report: (OECD, 2021)

Examples:

  • Education funding has increased (Department of Education, 2021).
  • The World Bank (2021) reports on global poverty trends.

Government Reports

▼

Format: Government Department. (Year). Report title. Publisher.

Example: Department of Health. (2021). Mental health services review. HMSO.

With Report Number: Office for National Statistics. (2021). Population statistics 2021 (Report No. ONS-2021-04). HMSO.

International Organisation Reports

▼

Format: Organisation. (Year). Report title. Publisher/URL.

Examples:

World Health Organisation. (2021). Global health statistics. WHO Press.

OECD. (2021). Education at a glance 2021. OECD Publishing. doi:10.1787/b35a14e5-en

Think Tank and NGO Reports

▼

Format: Organisation. (Year). Report title. Organisation Name.

Examples:

Brookings Institution. (2021). Economic policy recommendations. Brookings Institution.

Oxfam. (2021). Global inequality report 2021. Oxfam International. https://www.oxfam.org/inequality-report
